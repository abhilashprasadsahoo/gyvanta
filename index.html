ABOUT GYVANTA

Est.2022Bhubaneswar, Odisha
🚀GROWTH FIRST

Gyvanta was born from a simple belief — every Odia business deserves world-class digital marketing. We started in Bhubaneswar with a handful of clients and a big vision: to put Odisha brands on the national and global map.

Today we are a full-stack social media marketing agency managing 50+ brands across industries — retail, hospitality, education, healthcare, real estate, and beyond. Our team combines creative storytelling with hard data to deliver campaigns that genuinely move the needle.

We are proud to be rooted in Odisha and equally proud of the results we deliver — millions of impressions, thousands of leads, and lasting brand equity for our clients.

No. All our plans are month-to-month. We earn your trust by delivering results, not by locking you in.

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, YouTube, and Google Business Profile. We recommend the best mix based on your industry.

How soon will I see results?

Organic growth typically shows within 60–90 days. Paid campaigns can produce leads within the first week of going live.

Absolutely. While we specialise in the Odia market, we work with clients across India. Our strategies are tailored to your target audience, wherever they are.
